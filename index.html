About Us

Built On The Lot, Not In A Boardroom

Platinum Motors started as a two-bay garage and grew into a full-service classic car dealership because we couldn't stop chasing good cars. We source vehicles directly from long-time owners and estates, verify their history, and prepare every one of them the way we'd want a car prepared for our own driveway. No auction gambles, no surprises — just cars we're proud to stand behind.
